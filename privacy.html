Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

rclone-app is a private OAuth client used by a single person (its owner) with the open-source tool rclone. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope. It can access only files that the app itself uploaded to the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read any other files.

How data is used

Files are uploaded to and downloaded from the owner's own Google Drive. No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with anyone else, and no analytics or advertising is used. The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's devices and on temporary servers rented by the owner, which are deleted after use.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
